Get in touch

01Open the app and save it to your phone

Use the staff link on your phone. Save it to your home screen once so it opens like an app.

  1. On your phone, open bhlwebmaster.github.io/staff_time in Chrome or Safari.
  2. iPhone: tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Android: tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen.
  3. Everyone is shown as a card with their photo, status and today's hours. Tap your own name.

At the top: the BHL logo or Staff brings you back to this screen. Admin is for admins and finance only. Help opens this guide.

TipThe big green clock is UK time, which your schedule uses. The small grey clock is Manila time, for reference.

02Create your PIN (first time only)

Your 4-digit PIN stops anyone else clocking in as you. You make it yourself the first time you tap your name.

  1. Tap your name. The first time, it asks you to Create a 4-digit PIN.
  2. Type 4 digits, then type them again to confirm.
  3. From then on, tap your name and enter your PIN each time.
Heads upAfter 5 wrong PINs your name locks for 15 minutes. Forgot it? Ask an admin to reset it, then make a new one.

03Clock in (and use your OT)

One tap. The app uses its own clock, not your phone's, so the time is always right.

  1. Check Today's schedule. It's your shift in UK time, from the weekly schedule.
  2. Have OT to use? A green box shows it, e.g. "You have 1:30 OT from Thu, 24 Sept." Pick Start later or Leave earlier to use it today, or No. Your times and a note are filled in for you. If you have an OT bank (see Out-of-hours OT), you also choose how much to use.
  3. Working shifted hours for another reason? Open Different hours today? and set the start and end. Add a Note if needed.
  4. Tap Clock in. You'll see the time, your XP and your streak.
Good to knowIf you picked No, your OT still covers you if you end up leaving a little early today. Usually it expires at the end of today; an OT bank keeps it until you use it.

04Start and end lunch

The big button always shows your next step. After clocking in, it turns amber for lunch.

  1. When you go on break, tap Start lunch. When you're back, tap End lunch.
  2. Only the lunch you tap is taken off your hours. No lunch today? Just don't tap it.
Please tap itIf you take a break but forget to tap, it counts as work time and the admin sees "No lunch logged".

05Clock out

Tap Clock out at the end of your shift. Leaving before your end time? The app asks you to tap again so you don't do it by mistake.

  1. End your lunch first (the app reminds you).
  2. Tap Clock out. Before your scheduled end, tap a second time to confirm.
  3. Your day fills in: time in, lunch out, lunch in and time out, in UK and Manila time.
Forgot to clock out?Tell an admin with the right time. They can fix it, and every change is recorded.

06Tell the WhatsApp group

Only at the start and end of your shift. No message is needed for lunch.

  1. After you clock in (and again after you clock out), the green button Copy & Send to WhatsApp lights up.
  2. Tap it. Your message is copied in the usual format: Date / Name / Sched / Time In / Time out (plus any Pre-shift / Post-shift OT lines).
  3. Open the BHL Attendance group in WhatsApp, paste and press Send.

My payslips is right next to it (see See your payslips).

07Your hours and OT

Simple rules: you're paid for the time you actually work, and extra time can be used the next work day.

  • Worked = time out − time in − the lunch you tapped.
  • Expected = your schedule for that day minus its planned lunch (e.g. 9:00–6:00 with 1-hour lunch = 8:00). No lunch taken? Then it's the shift length, but never more than your normal day: a 9:00–6:00 day is still 8:00, a 9:00–1:00 half day is 4:00.
  • OT counts in 30-minute blocks: 30 min extra = 0:30, 45 min = 0:30, 1:20 = 1:00, 1:30 = 1:30.
  • Use OT on your next work day only (start later or leave earlier). Rest days, leave and holidays are skipped, so Friday's OT works on Monday.
  • Unused OT expires at the end of that day. It isn't paid and doesn't carry over. (People with Out-of-hours OT have an OT bank instead.)
  • Short: time you're under your expected hours that your OT can't cover. It counts as undertime on your payslip.
  • Late only shows if your day ends up short. Worked your full hours? No late mark.
OT to use todayThe This month card shows how much OT you can still use today. With an OT bank, the My OT bank card at the top shows it too, with the next cut-off.

08Out-of-hours OT (called in outside your shift)

For urgent work before your shift starts, after you've clocked out, or on your rest day. Only for people an admin turns it on for. Everyone else keeps the usual OT rules.

  1. Open the app and enter your PIN. Under the Clock in / Clock out card is the Out-of-hours OT card.
  2. Type who asked and what for, e.g. "James: fix checkout email".
  3. Called in before your shift (e.g. 1 PM Manila when you start at 4 PM)? Tap Start pre-shift OT. Called in after you've clocked out? Tap Start post-shift OT. Called in on your rest day? Tap Start rest-day OT.
  4. Tap End OT when you're done. Do this every time you're called in; you can have several sessions in a day.
  5. Pre-shift OT? End it first, then Clock in as usual when your shift starts.
  • Only the button that fits is active: pre-shift before you clock in, post-shift after you clock out. During your shift they're greyed out. On a rest day there's one button, Start rest-day OT.
  • The app uses its own clock and keeps your note, so there's a clear record of what you did and why.
  • The day's time (shift + out-of-hours) is added up, then counted in 30-minute blocks. Two 20-minute calls = 0:30 OT.
  • Your OT carries over until you use it, in your OT bank. It doesn't expire and it isn't paid.
  • The My OT bank card at the top of your screen shows how much you have, the next cut-off (15th or end of month) and how many days are left. Try to use your bank before each cut-off so it doesn't pile up.
  • At clock-in, choose how much of your bank to use (start later or leave earlier). You can use up to 3 hours a day (the most you can is picked for you; choose less if you like). The rest stays banked for another day.
  • Any other short time (e.g. a half day that ends early) comes out of your bank too. Only when the bank is empty does the rest count as Short (undertime on your payslip).
  • On a rest day, every minute counts as OT. Use Start rest-day OT for each call, or Clock in / Clock out for a longer stretch.
  • On leave or a public holiday the buttons are off. If you're called in, ask an admin to add the OT.
Forgot to tap End OT?It stops by itself after 4 hours and an admin checks the time. Tell an admin the right end time.

09Shift reminders on your phone

Get a notification before your shift starts, a nudge to take your lunch break, and one before your shift ends.

  1. iPhone: add the app to your home screen first (Share → Add to Home Screen) and open it from there. Android: Chrome works as is.
  2. Enter your PIN. In the Shift reminders card, tap Turn on reminders and allow notifications.
  3. Tap Show a test to see how they look.
  • Ready to start work? 30 minutes before your shift (not if you've already clocked in).
  • Time for a break: 4 hours after your start, a reminder to rest and take lunch. Tap OK: it doesn't start your lunch, you still tap Start lunch in the app.
  • Time to wrap up: 30 minutes before your shift ends, to make sure today's work is done.
  • Working days only. Times follow your shift that day (start later / leave earlier included). An admin can change the times and messages.
New phone or someone else's?Reminders are per phone. Turn them on again on each phone you use, and Turn off on a phone you share.

10The team schedule on the home screen

Below the name cards is the team's schedule in UK time. It opens on Today.

  • Today shows just today's shifts, with a Status column: Working, On lunch, Clocked out, Not in yet, Late · not in, Absent, Rest day, Vacation, Sick, Emergency leave or a holiday.
  • This week shows Sunday to Saturday. Yellow = rest day, green = vacation, orange = emergency leave, blue = holiday (with its name).
  • Table or Timeline: the timeline shows each person as one coloured bar, like a calendar.
  • Use ← / → to move a day (or a week). Tap Today or This week again to jump back to now.

11My schedule and asking for changes

After your PIN, My schedule shows this week and the next 2 weeks. You can ask to change your own days.

  1. Pick This week, Next week or the week after. Today is highlighted.
  2. Tap Request a change. For each upcoming day choose Working (with times), Rest Day, Vacation, Sick, Emergency Leave or Unpaid Leave.
  3. Add a short note for the admin and tap Send for approval.
  4. Your changes show in orange with Waiting for admin approval. You can edit or withdraw until an admin decides.
After a decisionApproved: the schedule updates for everyone. Not approved: you'll see the admin's message. Changes an admin makes show here straight away.

12See your payslips

Pay is every cut-off (1st–15th and 16th–end). Once payroll is finalised, your payslip is in the app. Only you can see it, with your PIN.

  1. Tap your name and enter your PIN.
  2. Tap My payslips (next to the WhatsApp button).
  3. Pick a Pay period: see your basic pay, deductions and the peso amount you receive.
  4. Tap Download PDF for a full-page copy with The Biohack Group header.
Something looks wrong?Message the Company Human Resources with the pay period and what doesn't match.

13Make it yours: avatar, photo, colour, tagline

Tap your avatar (the small Edit tag) or Edit profile. Everyone sees your card on the home screen.

  1. Pick an avatar or tap Upload your photo (cropped and shrunk automatically).
  2. Choose a card colour and add a tagline (up to 40 characters).
  3. Tap Save profile.
Good to knowKeep photos work-appropriate. Admins can remove a photo.

15Problems and answers

I forgot my PIN
Ask an admin to reset it. Next time you tap your name, make a new 4-digit PIN.
It says "Too many wrong PINs"
After 5 wrong tries your name is locked for 15 minutes. Wait, then try again, or ask an admin to reset your PIN.
I forgot to clock out, or clocked in at the wrong time
Send an admin the correct time (e.g. "Clocked out 3:05 PM UK yesterday"). You can't change past days yourself; this keeps records fair.
I can't see my name
An admin needs to add you first under Staff. Then refresh the page.
The page won't load or looks broken
Check your internet, then pull down to refresh (Ctrl+R / Cmd+R on a computer). Still stuck? Tell an admin, and post your times in WhatsApp for now.
Where did my OT go?
OT can only be used on your next work day, then it expires. Check OT to use today on the This month card. If you couldn't use it for a good reason, ask an admin: they can add it back to a day. With an OT bank (Out-of-hours OT), it stays until you use it: check OT in your bank.
I forgot to tap End OT
It stops by itself 4 hours after it started, and admins see an OT auto-stopped flag. Send an admin the real end time (e.g. "Ended pre-shift OT at 2:10 PM Manila") and they'll fix it.
I can't see the Out-of-hours OT card
It only shows for people an admin has turned it on for. Ask an admin if you're often called in outside your shift.
I didn't take lunch today
That's fine: don't tap lunch. Your full time counts. If your schedule has no lunch on that day (e.g. part-time), ask an admin to set lunch to 0 so your expected hours are right.
My shift today is wrong
Your shift comes from the weekly schedule. For today, open Different hours today? before clocking in. For future days, use Request a change in My schedule.
I can't find my payslip
Payslips show only after payroll for that cut-off is finalised. If a finalised period is missing, message an admin.
Why doesn't my phone's time matter?
The app uses its own clock, so changing your phone's time makes no difference. It keeps everyone's records fair.

01Sign in, and who can do what

Admins and finance use the admin page with an email and password.

  1. Open bhlwebmaster.github.io/staff_time/admin.html and bookmark it.
  2. Sign in with the email and password you were given.
  3. Got a temporary password? Change it under Access → My password.

The Staff / Admin switch at the top jumps between the staff app and this page.

Two rolesAdmin can do everything. Finance can view Today, Timesheets, Reports, Schedule and Payroll and download PDFs and CSVs, but can't change anything.

02The live Today board

Who's working, on lunch, clocked out, not in yet, off or on leave. It refreshes every minute.

  • Totals at the top: Working · On lunch · Clocked out · Not in · Late. People on a rest day, leave or a holiday aren't counted as Not in.
  • Flags: Late, OT +x, OT used (OT covering today), Short, No clock-out and No lunch logged. Staff notes show under the flags.
  • Out-of-hours OT people also get Out-of-hours x:xx (time clocked before or after the shift), On out-of-hours OT now, OT auto-stopped (nobody tapped End OT: check the time) and OT bank … over cap.
  • Use the Day picker to look at any past date.

03Fix or add an entry

For a missed clock-out, a wrong time, a day someone forgot to log, or an OT exception.

  1. On Today or in Timesheets → Daily entries, click Edit (or Add / + Add entry).
  2. Change the times (UK) or note and click Save. Times must run in order: in → lunch out → lunch in → out.
  3. Changing the Sched start/end here makes it that day's planned schedule (an admin decision).
  4. Extra OT credit (minutes): lets someone use OT that already expired, e.g. 60 if they were too busy to use it.
  5. Out-of-hours OT (people with it turned on): add, fix or delete the day's pre-shift and post-shift sessions (start, end, note). Each session saves on its own with its Save button. An OT auto-stopped flag means nobody tapped End OT, so check the end time.
HistoryEvery create, edit and delete is logged with who did it and when. It's shown at the bottom of the dialog.

04Timesheets

Hours per person for any period, ready for payroll or invoicing.

  1. Pick This week, Last week, This month, Last month, or set From / To.
  2. Click a person's row to filter the daily entries to them. The name column stays in place when you scroll sideways.
  3. Download Summary CSV or Daily CSV for Excel/Sheets, or use Print / PDF.

Columns: OT earned, OT used (OT spent on a later work day), Short (time under expected hours that OT didn't cover), Late, Billable hrs (regular + OT earned) and OT bank (what Out-of-hours OT people have banked now; ⚠ when over the cap). The CSVs also have out-of-hours OT hours.

Hours per person by day, week or month, or across any dates.

06Add and manage staff

Staff don't need an email, just their name and a PIN they create themselves.

  1. Staff → + Add person. Enter the Display name and the Timesheet name (e.g. GAMOTAN, VERGE).
  2. Set their usual Start / End (UK) and standard unpaid lunch.
  3. Usual week: tick the working days. Each day has its own start, end and Lunch (min). Use 0 for no-lunch days (part-time, or a no-lunch day to leave early on another day).
  4. For payroll: Start date, Pay type and rate (see Payroll).
  5. Out-of-hours OT (only for people who get called in outside their shift, e.g. Verge and Cess): tick it and set OT bank starts on. They get the Out-of-hours OT card, and their OT carries over until used.
  6. Click Save and send them the staff link.
  • Forgotten PIN: Edit → Reset PIN. Unsuitable photo: Edit → Remove photo.
  • Someone leaves: set Status to Inactive. Their history stays.

07Weekly schedule and staff requests

The usual week comes from Staff → Edit. Change single days here. Everyone sees it on the home screen.

  1. Open Schedule. Change any cell to a different shift, Rest Day, Vacation, Sick, Emergency Leave, Holiday or Unpaid Leave. Changed days are outlined in orange.
  2. Click Save week. Copy last week and Reset to usual week help.
  3. Staff requests: an orange number on the Schedule tab means requests are waiting. Each shows the days, what's planned now and what they want, plus their note.
  4. Type an optional message, then Approve (the schedule updates straight away) or Reject (they see your message).
Good to knowPublic holidays show automatically. Finance can see requests but can't approve.

08How hours and OT work (Settings)

One set of rules for everyone. Change them under Settings.

TermRule
WorkedTime out − time in − the lunch they tapped. No lunch tapped = nothing taken off.
ExpectedThat day's planned schedule minus its planned lunch (Staff → Usual week). If no lunch was tapped: the shift length, capped at their normal day (9:00–6:00 → 8:00; a 9:00–1:00 half day → 4:00, not 3:00).
OTWorked − expected, in whole blocks (default 30 min: 45 → 0:30, 1:20 → 1:00).
OT useOnly on the next work day (the next clock-in within 4 days, so Friday → Monday). Unused OT expires. Staff choose Start later / Leave earlier at clock-in.
Out-of-hours OTOnly for people with Out-of-hours OT ticked (Staff → Edit). They clock pre-shift OT (before clocking in, working days), post-shift OT (after clocking out) and rest-day OT with a note; the minutes are added to that day's worked time before rounding. A session nobody ends stops after 4 hours and is flagged.
OT bankFor those same people: OT carries over until used (no expiry, not paid). They pick how much to use at clock-in, up to the Max OT bank use per day (Settings, default 3 h). Any other short time is taken from the whole bank; it's undertime only once the bank is empty. Above the OT bank cap (Settings, default 8 h) it's still banked but flagged in Timesheets.
ShortUnder expected hours after OT is used. Counts as undertime in payroll.
LateAfter start + grace (5 min). With Judge by hours on, a day with full hours isn't late.
No lunch loggedA day with a planned lunch but no taps. Worth a quick check.
BillableRegular hours (capped at expected) + OT earned.

Shift reminders (Settings): turn each reminder on or off and set its minutes, title and message. Send a test to all phones checks it works. Under the buttons you can see who has reminders on. First-time setup is in the README (Reminders).

Settings also has: Count minutes before scheduled start (on), Judge by hours (on), OT bank cap (8 hours), Max OT bank use per day (3 hours) and Working days per cut-off (10).

09Public holidays (Philippines)

The official 2026 and 2027 lists are already loaded under Settings → Public holidays.

  • A holiday on someone's working day becomes a paid day off. It shows by name on the schedule and counts in payroll. On a rest day it changes nothing.
  • Add new dates (e.g. Eid'l Fitr / Eid'l Adha for 2027 when announced) or Remove one.
  • Untick Paid for a no-work-no-pay special day.
  • To have someone work a holiday, change their day in the Schedule tab.

10Payroll and payslips

Pay is bi-monthly: 1st–15th and 16th–end. Everything is worked out from attendance, the schedule and holidays.

  1. Once per person (Staff → Edit): Start date, Pay type and rate.
  2. Payroll → + New period: pick the Cut-off (dates fill in), check the payment date, add the exchange rate (₱ per £1) and total transfer fee (₱).
  3. Check the sheet. Days, hours, lates, undertime and absences fill in automatically. Type over any number to change it (it turns orange; ↺ undoes). Add Other deductions (CA, loans, taxes) with a note.
  4. Save draft any time. When it's right, Finalise & publish (twice). Staff then see their payslips.
  5. Pick an Employee name and Download this payslip (PDF), or All payslips PDF (one A4 page each), or CSV.

Pay types

Pay typePay per cut-off
Hourly(Regular hours worked + OT used + paid leave/holiday hours) × hourly rate. Lates aren't deducted again (they're already missing from the hours).
Daily(Days worked + paid leave/holiday days) × daily rate. Days not worked aren't paid.
WeeklyDaily rate = weekly ÷ 5; pay = daily × 10. £150/week → £300.
Bi-monthlyThe rate is the pay per cut-off. Daily rate = rate ÷ 10.
MonthlyHalf the monthly rate each cut-off. £600/month → £300.

Deductions and totals

ItemRule
UndertimeShort time not covered by the last work day's OT × per-minute rate (daily rate ÷ paid minutes per day).
AbsencesPlanned shifts with no clock-in (up to yesterday) and unpaid leave. Weekly / Bi-monthly / Monthly: deducted at the daily rate. Hourly / Daily: simply not paid.
Paid days offVacation, sick, emergency leave and public holidays.
New starterFixed pay types are paid per working day from their start date, up to the full cut-off.
Mixed pay typesEveryone uses their own type and rate. Totals and the fee split work on everyone together.
PesosNet pay × exchange rate. The transfer fee is split by each person's share of total net pay.

The payslip PDF is a full A4 page: The Biohack Group logo, employee and pay details, earnings, deductions, net pay and the amount received in pesos. The footer says it's computer-generated (no signature needed), asks staff to report discrepancies to the Company Human Resources, and names the Employer.

Good to knowOnly admins create, edit or finalise payroll. Finance can view and download. To change a finalised period, click Reopen to edit; it's hidden from staff until you finalise again.

Only admins can do this.

  1. Access → Create a login: enter the email and pick Finance or Admin.
  2. Click Generate, then Create login, then Copy details and send them privately.
  3. Change a role with the dropdown, or Remove (twice) to take access away.

12Problems and answers

Someone forgot their PIN or is locked out
Staff → Edit → Reset PIN. They make a new PIN next time. (Locked after 5 wrong tries? They can also wait 15 minutes.)
I can't sign in
"Invalid login credentials": wrong email or password; another admin can remove and re-create your login. "This login doesn't have access": ask an admin to add you under Access.
An out-of-hours OT session has the wrong time, or says auto-stopped
Open that day (Edit). Under Out-of-hours OT, change the start or end time and click that session's Save. Use Add session if someone forgot to clock it, with a note of who asked.
Someone's OT expired but they couldn't use it
Open the day they want to use it (Edit) and set Extra OT credit, e.g. 60 minutes.
"No lunch logged" on a day
They had a planned lunch but didn't tap it, so the time counts as worked. If they did take a break, edit the day and add the lunch times. If that day never has lunch, set its Lunch to 0 in Staff → Usual week.
A part-timer shows Short every day
Check their Usual week: each day's times and Lunch (min) should match what they really work (e.g. 7:00–11:00 with lunch 0 = 4:00).
Payroll absences or days look wrong
Check the Schedule for those dates (a missing rest day or leave shows as an absence) and Timesheets for missing clock-ins. Then reopen the period.
A payroll number is orange
Someone typed over the automatic value. Click ↺ to go back.
A person has left the team
Staff → Edit → Status Inactive. Their past hours stay in reports.
For the webmaster (technical)
How do I update the app?
GitHub (bhlwebmaster/staff_time) → Add file → Upload files, drag in the changed files, Commit changes. Never upload over config.js. If a change needs the database, run supabase/database-update.sql in Supabase → SQL Editor first (safe to re-run).
"This app isn't set up yet"
config.js on GitHub is missing the Supabase Project URL / publishable key, or was replaced by a blank copy.
"Could not find the … column … in the schema cache" or "violates check constraint"
The database is behind the site. Run all of supabase/database-update.sql in Supabase → SQL Editor.
Supabase Security Advisor warnings
supabase/security.sql (included in database-update.sql) moves privileged functions to a private schema. "Leaked Password Protection" is a paid-plan setting and can be left on the free plan.
Creating a login fails
Supabase → Edge Functions: admin-users must be deployed with "Verify JWT" off.
The app stopped loading after a quiet week
The free plan pauses after about a week with no use. Sign in to Supabase (biohack.webmaster@gmail.com) and click Restore.
