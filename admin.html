Rules

Settings

Pay is bi-monthly (1st–15th, 16th–end). For Weekly, Bi-monthly and Monthly pay, the daily rate = pay per cut-off ÷ this number. Absences, lates and undertime are deducted from that.

Worked = time out − time in − the lunch they tapped (no lunch tapped = nothing taken off). Expected = that day's schedule minus its planned lunch (Staff → Usual week). Extra time is OT in whole blocks (30 min: 45 extra → 30, 1:20 extra → 1:00). Less time shows as Short and is covered by OT first.

Philippines

Public holidays

On a holiday, anyone scheduled to work gets a paid day off. It shows on the schedule and in payroll automatically. Untick Paid for a no-work-no-pay day. To have someone work on a holiday, change their day in the Schedule tab.
